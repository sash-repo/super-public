# cssei

**New simple modular Lie (super)algebras**

D. Leites, O. Lozhechnyk. Status: in preparation.

The programs behind the computed claims of the paper: the dimensions at N = (1,...,1) of the
simple vectorial Lie algebras of Bouarroudj-Grozman-Lebedev-Leites-Shchepochkina
(arXiv:1510.07255, Tables 25.2 and 25.5) and of their superizations; the Z/2-gradings and the two
"method 2" superizations s_a, s_b of Shen's algebra gs(2); a random search for simple Lie algebras
over F_2 after Eick, with the invariants of Cushing-Stagg-Stewart (arXiv:2207.01094, Table C.3)
of the constituents found; the two series T(N;g); the algebras P_1(6), P_2(6) of
Kondrateva-Kuznetsov; sandwich subalgebras; Weisfeiler filtrations. Everything is exact
arithmetic over F_2 or over GF(2^K). Nothing here uses Mathematica or SuperLie.

## How to run

```bash
python3 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install -r cssei/requirements.txt
cd cssei
../venv/bin/python <script>.py <arguments>
```

Run every script from inside this folder (the path `sub/script.py` is given relative to it)
unless the table says "in `sub/`". The scripts find `../lib/exactlin.py` and one another by
themselves. numba compiles the inner loops on the first call (a few seconds).

The times are those of one run on an Apple M-series laptop (10 cores) without other load; most
claims take seconds to minutes. Some claims need hours or days (exhaustive searches); for them
the table gives the command and the time, and a shorter run of the same command that checks a
part of the claim.

Some scripts write their results into `results/` (created when needed) or next to their input;
these files are not part of the deposit.

## Data in this folder

All data are small (the folder is about 5 MB) and are inputs, not results:

- `blocks/k<d>_<nil>_<tor>.npz`, `new11/k*.json`, `cmp/r15.json`, `cmp/sbt7.json`: the structure
  tables of the eleven simple constituents of Table (2). They are regenerated from one seed each
  by `blocks/regen.py` (see the table), the tables `new11/k*.json` from them by `new11/export.py`.
- `blocks/g2029944.npz` (dim 60), `g2037408.npz`, `g2046526.npz` (the two of dim 63),
  `g2055742.npz` (dim 123), `y45.npz` (dim 45): the constituents of dim > 31 of Table (18), with
  the Z-grading inherited from the ambient algebra; regenerated by `blocks/graded.py SEED OUT.npz`
  from the seed in the name (`y45.npz` is the dim-45 constituent of seed 2004184 in a coarser
  grading, 5 degrees). `blocks/cal_hI5.npz`, `cal_k5.npz`: calibration tables.
- `rand/found/*.npz`: one structure table per fingerprint found by the random search;
  `rand/tabs/*.npz`: the simple cores of the rows of Table (15) used as ambient algebras
  (`rand/export_cores.py`); `rand/new30.json`: the 30-dimensional simple core of the Cartan prolong
  of (V, gs(2)) for the 8-dimensional irreducible gs(2)-module V, used as an ambient algebra;
  `rand/css_inv.json`: the invariants of the algebras of the library of [CSS] (`rand/ident.py`).
- `kk/P1_6.json`, `kk/P2_6.json`: P_1(6), P_2(6) (the tables `P1`, `P2` that `kk/kk.py` writes to
  `kk_tables.json`); `kk/P<m>_<N>.json`: the nine P(m;N), |N| = 6 (`kk/pexport.py m N`, e.g. `pexport.py 2 3,3`);
  `kk/iso/*.json`: T(N;g) of dim 63 (`kk/iso/tex63.py`) and W(1;[6])' (`kk/iso/w16.py`).
- `osp/s_gs2.json`: the two superizations of gs(2) as matrix superalgebras (`osp/export_s.py`);
  `osp/b_w.npz`, `osp/bgl1_w.npz`: b_w(2;1) and bgl^(1)(3;w) over GF(4) (`osp/export_b.py`,
  `osp/cmp_bgl.py`).
- `cmp/*.json`: structure tables for the counts (`cmp/export.py`, `cmp/export_series.py`).
- `css-lib/repo/`: the bases and thin tables of the library of Cushing, Stagg and Stewart,
  github.com/cushydom88/simple-lie-algebras (MIT license, `css-lib/repo/LICENSE`); only the data
  directories `SimpleLieAlgebraBases/` and `ThinTables/` are kept.
