# borvinrel

**Vinberg's theorem, Borcherds superalgebras, and presentations**

P. Grozman (deceased), A. Krutov, A. Lebedev, D. Leites, O. Lozhechnyk. Status: in preparation.

The scripts compute, for Lie (super)algebras g(A) with Cartan matrix A: the censuses of the
symmetric (and symmetrizable) integer matrices of type Pos and of the partial Cartan matrices,
with their lattices, graphs and the Weyl group elements they carry; the odd reflections and the
inequivalent Cartan matrices of a Lie superalgebra; the defining relations of g(A) as the radical
of the contravariant (Shapovalov) form, stratified by the irreducible factors of its determinant
with the entries of A kept symbolic; and the dimensions of the homogeneous components of g(A),
of its even part and of the subalgebras generated by brackets of odd generators. Everything is
exact: integers and fractions, sympy over Q(a, b, c), `../lib/exactlin.py` (python-flint when
installed), and, in `evenpart/` and `shuhanrel/`, arithmetic modulo large primes in which the
square roots of the irrational Cartan entries exist (each run is repeated over two primes).

## How to run

```bash
python3 -m venv venv && venv/bin/pip install -r borvinrel/requirements.txt
cd borvinrel
../venv/bin/python reflections.py
```

Every script is run from inside the folder it lies in (`borvinrel/` or one of its subfolders);
it finds `../lib/exactlin.py` and the shared engines by itself. Three scripts write a pickle that
a later one reads: `par8.py` writes `level8.pkl` (read by `graphs8.py`), `graphs8.py` writes
`graphs8.pkl` (read by `carter3.py`), `par9bip.py 10` writes `bip9.pkl`, `bip10.pkl` (read by
`latbip.py`). The engines of the stratification are `shapovalov.py` (free Lie superalgebra,
lowering operators, Gram matrix of the contravariant form) and `driver.py` (the recursion on the
walls).

Several scripts also write their output to a log file (`*.log`, `log-*.txt`) or a pickle in the
folder they run in.

The times given were measured on a machine running many other jobs at once; on an idle laptop
they are several times shorter. Runs not marked with a time take under three minutes.

not reproduced here:
